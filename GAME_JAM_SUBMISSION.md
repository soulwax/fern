## 📜 The Folklore & Premise

In German, **"fern"** means *distant*, *far away*, or *foreign*—the root of *Fernweh* (the ache for the mysterious distant horizon). Simultaneously, *der Farn* is the mythical fern of Central European and Germanic folklore:

> *According to Black Forest and Bohemian legends, the mythical Fern Flower (Farnblume) blooms only once a year on Midsummer Eve at the stroke of midnight. It burns with an ethereal cold blue-violet fire, granting its bearer the sight to perceive hidden spirits and understand the speech of the woods. Yet the sacred bloom invites the wrath of the ancient, unseen guardians of the deep forest.*

You are an herbalist and woodcarver who ventured into the dark Black Forest at midnight and plucked the glowing **Farnblume**. You bolted your heavy timber workshop door behind you, but you did not return alone. The guardian of the woods—an ancient, invisible forest wraith—has seeped inside through the rafters. 

You must survive inside your workshop from **Midnight (00:00)** until the valley church bells toll across the mist at **Dawn (06:00)**.

## 👁️ Surviving an Invisible Threat: Sensory Workshop Mechanics

The forest wraith is completely invisible to the naked human eye. You cannot fight it with brute force—you survive by interpreting the tactile environment of your carpenter's workshop:

### 1. 🌸 The Farnblume (UV Lantern of Truth)
- Aiming your handheld bioluminescent blossom directly at the entity casts a pale violet-cyan light cone that exposes its horrifying true form: a towering monstrosity of gnarled bark, twisted roots, and crown of stag antlers.
- *Cost:* The petals wilt and lose luminescence if exposed continuously. You must cup your hands (<kbd>R</kbd>) to preserve its vigor, or dip it into earthenware jars of mountain spring water placed around the workshop.

### 2. 🪵 Sawdust & Shavings Footprints
- The workshop floorboards are blanketed in dry wood shavings and sawdust. When the unseen creature prowls, cloven hoofprints depress into the sawdust in real-time, accompanied by the dry *crunch-crunch-crunch* of pine needles and timber.

### 3. 🕯️ Workbench Candles (*Der Alp* Mechanic)
- Warm amber candlelight illuminates the benches. When the entity prowls nearby, it snuffs candle flames with a ghostly breath, plunging your defensive sightlines into pitch darkness. Strike a match (<kbd>E</kbd>) to relight them.

### 4. 🪜 Loft Ladder & Catwalk Verticality
- Climb the wooden ladder (<kbd>E</kbd>) to access elevated rafter catwalks. Survey the workshop floor to spot cloven hoofprints from safety—but beware, the wraith also stalks along the ceiling beams!

### 5. ✨ The Grindstone Spark Shower
- Pedaling the heavy iron grindstone sprays a fiery fan of sparks across the room. Any sparks that strike the invisible entity outline its silhouette in sizzling embers, stunning it for 2.5 seconds.

### 6. ⛓️ Hanging Chains & Pulleys
- Suspended iron hooks and pulleys swing violently and creak on their iron mountings whenever the unseen entity glides beneath them.

### 7. 🔨 Timber Window Barricades
- When shutters rattle under the wraith's assault, hammer timber planks across the breach to delay and repel its intrusion.

### 8. ⭐ *Drudenfuss* Chalk Runes
- Inscribe traditional Germanic pentagram runes across thresholds to form a sacred barrier that repels the spirit for 35 seconds.

### 9. 🔔 Concussive Iron Anvil Strike
- Strike the central workshop anvil with an iron mallet to unleash a 14-meter concussive acoustic shockwave that stuns the wraith's sensitive hearing.

## 🕹️ Controls Reference

| Input | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| <kbd>W</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> | Move (Walk) |
| <kbd>Left Shift</kbd> | Sprint (Consumes stamina) |
| <kbd>Mouse</kbd> | Look / Aim |
| <kbd>E</kbd> (Hold / Tap) | Interact with Workshop Stations (Grindstone, Barricade, Anvil, Chalk, Candles, Ladder) |
| <kbd>R</kbd> | Cup Hands over Farnblume (Conserve luminescence) |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Pause Menu (Volume & Mouse Sensitivity sliders) |

## 🌙 Selectable Difficulty Presets

- **Midsummer Eve (Standard):** The canonical Gothic horror experience. 6-hour survival night with balanced bloom decay and standard wraith speed.
- **Walpurgisnacht (Nightmare):** High-stakes psychological terror: +25% wraith hunt speed, 1.5x flower wilt rate, wider candle snuffing, and hyper-sensitive auditory cues.
- **Stille Nacht (Story & Exploration):** Atmospheric story mode with -25% wraith speed and halved flower decay, designed for exploring the workshop, examining carpenter tools, and reading folklore.
